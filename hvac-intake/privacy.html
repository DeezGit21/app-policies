HVAC Intake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

HVAC Intake is a field tool used by authorized technicians of their employing HVAC company to collect customer information, perform diagnostic calculations, and prepare estimates during on-site service visits.

What we collect

  • Information entered by the technician during a service visit: customer name, service address, phone number, equipment details, diagnostic readings, and notes.
  • Optional voice dictation audio is processed on-device to produce text and is not stored or transmitted by the app as audio.

How it is used

  • Collected information belongs to and is managed by the HVAC company whose technician entered it. It is used to document service visits and prepare estimates for customers.
  • AI-powered features (such as summary generation) may send technician-entered text to a language-model service to produce a draft. We do not use this content for advertising or resale.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or share customer data with third parties for marketing.
  • We do not track your location in the background.
  • The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Data retention & control

Records created in the app are retained by the user's employing company. To request deletion of any record, contact the HVAC company you dealt with, or the developer below.
